About the data

A value is the change in the expected number of extinctions over 100 years per km² of land that changes in the pixel. Positive means more extinctions. Blank pixels have no data.

Zoomed out you see averaged overview levels; from zoom 6 the map is drawn from the full 1 arc-minute data. The map is drawn in the browser from the Zarr store by the life-metric library.

Paper: doi:10.1098/rstb.2023.0327. This copy: source.coop/tessera/life. Method and code: quantifyearth/LIFE.
